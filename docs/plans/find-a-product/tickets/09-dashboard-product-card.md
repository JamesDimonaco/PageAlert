# 09: Product card on the dashboard

**What to build:** In the dashboard's monitor list, a product shows as one card with the most useful up-to-date facts: cheapest current price and where, whether any listing is in stock, and when it was last checked. A dropdown on the card shows its child monitors. Clicking the card opens the product view. Clicking a child opens that monitor.

**Blocked by:** 07

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Products appear in the same list as monitors, not on a separate page.
- [ ] The card shows the product name, number of listings watched, cheapest price with its site, an in-stock indicator, and last checked time.
- [ ] Child monitors appear under the card's dropdown, not as separate top-level cards.
- [ ] Each child monitor's own page shows a tag linking back to its product.
- [ ] The card updates live as checks come in, like the current monitor cards.
- [ ] A product with paused or errored children shows that on the card.
